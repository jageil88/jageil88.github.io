Privacy Policy

Durchgespielt Uploader · last updated 3 November 2026

Who uses this app

Durchgespielt Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @durchgespielt.de1 to publish that account's own videos. It is not offered to other people.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok account information (user ID, display name, avatar) to confirm the connected account.
  • An access token from TikTok Login Kit, used only to upload and publish videos to the connected account.
  • The creator's posting settings (for example, privacy options) returned by the Content Posting API.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Local tokens are then deleted.
